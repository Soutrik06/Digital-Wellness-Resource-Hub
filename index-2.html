Browser + Productivity

Creating a Distraction-Proof YouTube & Web Study Space

Setup time: 4 mins

YouTube is mandatory for many college lectures and coding tutorials, but the recommendations feed and comments cause massive derailment. This guide pairs Unhook with uBlock Origin for completely sterile educational viewing.

1

Install Unhook on Chrome, Firefox, or Edge

Visit your browser extension store and install "Unhook - Remove YouTube Recommended Videos". It requires no account creation and does not monitor keystrokes.

Tip: Pin the Unhook extension icon to your toolbar for quick toggles during breaks.
2

Configure Minimalist Defaults

Open YouTube, click the Unhook icon, and toggle on:

  • Hide Home Feed (replaces homepage with an empty zen search bar)
  • Hide Video Sidebar (prevents autoplay wormholes)
  • Hide Comments (eliminates cognitive distraction)
  • Hide Shorts Tab (blocks algorithmic vertical video doomscroll)
3

Verify & Run a Test Study Session

Open any lecture video. You will observe only the video player and lecture notes. When your lecture ends, close the tab without encountering autoplay algorithmic rabbit holes.

Student Reflection: "Toggling off the home feed eliminated my habit of opening YouTube 'just to look at my subscriptions' before writing my papers."
Mobile + Sleep Hygiene

Automating Nighttime Grayscale & Bedtime Wind-Down

Setup time: 5 mins

Color stimulation triggers dopamine loops on Instagram, TikTok, and Reddit. Turning your phone screen into monochrome black-and-white drastically reduces phone appeal after 10:30 PM.

iOS (iPhone) Grayscale Routine

  1. Open Settings > Accessibility > Display & Text Size.
  2. Tap Color Filters, switch it ON, and select Grayscale.
  3. To toggle quickly: Go to Accessibility Shortcut & check Color Filters. Now triple-click your side power button to toggle B&W instantly!
  4. Automation: In Apple Shortcuts > Automation, schedule "Turn Color Filters On at 10:30 PM" and "Off at 7:00 AM".

Android Bedtime Mode Routine

  1. Open Settings > Digital Wellbeing & Parental Controls.
  2. Tap Bedtime mode (built-in natively on Android 10+).
  3. Select Based on a schedule (e.g., 10:30 PM – 7:00 AM).
  4. Check the boxes for Grayscale and Do Not Disturb.
  5. Optional: Customize DND to allow calls from favorite emergency contacts while silencing notification banners.
Sleep Impact: In peer testing, students reported average screen-in-bed time dropped from 48 minutes to under 12 minutes within 3 nights of enabling grayscale.
Open-Source + Privacy

Self-Hosted Screen Time Auditing with ActivityWatch

Setup time: 6 mins

Commercial trackers like RescueTime upload your window titles and browsing history to remote servers. ActivityWatch is 100% open-source, runs locally on your PC/Mac, and never transmits your study data.

1

Download & Run the Safe Executable

Head to activitywatch.net and download the binary for Windows, macOS, or Linux. Unzip and run `aw-qt`. An icon will appear in your system tray.

2

Optional: Install the Browser Watcher

To see which specific tabs or research papers you are viewing (rather than just "Chrome.exe"), install the official ActivityWatch browser extension from the Chrome/Firefox Web Store. It connects strictly to `localhost:5600`.

3

Review Your Daily Balance in the Dashboard

Right-click the tray icon and click Open Dashboard. You'll get an interactive timeline of active work time vs idle time, with zero ads and zero data leaks.
